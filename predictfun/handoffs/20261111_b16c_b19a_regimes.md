## Answer

- **The combined target makes the guard worse.** B16c trained the guard on the mean of our order's value at 1 s, 5 s and 60 s.
  - Both arms read WORSE on the tape-based decision referee: -0.094 c/sh (qc) and -0.072 (qd) per quoted moment, better on 1 / 8
    and 2 / 8 days.
  - The v3 replay is worse at every horizon: settle -$517 / -$364 (qc, J / X), +1 s -$270 / -$272, +60 s -$119 / -$166, with fewer
    fills (4,478 against 5,278 at J).
  - The 60 s value swings far more than the 1 s value, so it dominates a plain average. The model fits a minute of mostly
    unpredictable drift and loses the short-term selection that worked: 0 / 8 days better at 1 s and 5 s.
  - Combining horizons would need each scaled first, or separate predictions combined at the decision. Neither is promising given
    the next finding.
- **At the back of the queue our fills lose in every regime, favourable and calm included.**
  - On the tape (B19a A), a 5-sh order of ours at the back loses per filled share at 1 s: calm -1.78, adverse -1.73, favourable
    -1.63 c/sh.
  - Calm and favourable moments are less bad per moment (-0.12 and -0.09 against -0.38) only because we are filled less there (6%
    and 5% of moments against 22%).
  - The makers on the same tape earn most in favourable moments: top 5 +0.93 c/sh at 1 s (+0.87 at 60 s); even the rest +0.67.
  - In a favourable move, the benign fills go to the front of the queue. What reaches the back is the move that failed: the level
    eaten before a reversal.
  - **So we do not capture favourable moves: we hold the right side, but our fills there are the losing ones.**
- **The guard already selects well within each regime.** The moments it lets quote are worth about zero (calm -0.010, adverse
  +0.026, favourable -0.005 c/sh per moment) against -0.11 to -0.41 for the moments it skips.
  - It quotes favourable moments most: 15.3% of them, and 50% of all its quotes.
  - A regime-aware gate can only choose among moments that are worth about zero at the back of the queue. Its ceiling is small.
  - The sign of a fill is set by queue position: back about -1.7 c/sh, front +0.4 to +0.9.

## What follows

- **Do not build the regime-aware guard yet.** At the back of the queue every regime's fills lose about 1.7 c/sh. A better gate
  can only move quoted moments from about zero to slightly positive.
- **The lever is queue position.** Every maker group's fills earn in favourable and calm moments, and ours lose there. Ways to
  move forward:
  - stop the churn (soft pull + incremental size, on main) and rest continuously, so the queue ahead depletes;
  - stay after being filled (the top 5 re-hit 59.5% against 38.4%);
  - be first at a level: inside the spread when it is two or more ticks wide. That is 1.9% of the top 5's volume at +1.30 c/sh
    (B12). Joining a new level early did not give priority at our latency (strategy review rev 3).
- **Measure first, then design.** The live run with the churn fix shows how far we actually move up: queue reach at fill, the
  share of fills from sweeps, markouts by order age.

## Limits

- The label is an order at the back at every moment. Resting continuously would move it up, which this does not credit.
- Tape fills are whole-market fills.
- The replay's fill model is not validated and misses pressure adverse selection.
- B16c's target was chosen after B16 was seen, and B19a's regime thresholds are fixed choices.
- The 9 days were read before.
