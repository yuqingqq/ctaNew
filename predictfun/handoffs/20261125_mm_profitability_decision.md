# Predict.fun passive market-making decision, November 25

Use the buffered straight-line unwind as the reference for a single test of protecting resting orders before an adverse BTC price move. The present evidence supports a loss-reduction mechanism. Positive settlement P&L remains to be demonstrated.

## What pays and what costs money

The latest [smooth-unwind replay](../reviews/smooth_unwind_20261125/report.md) covers October 26, a consumed day. Of 288 scheduled markets, 70 have complete paths, 124 lack the endpoint and 94 have stale books. All 70 use the opening trigger, two levels of up to 220 shares, a 1,000-share hard cap and the same passive execution assumptions. The buffered straight line brought average absolute ending inventory down from 63.2 shares under the buffered staircase to 41.9. It completed 6,792 fewer matched shares and improved settlement by $540.60. Adding the tested market-dependent multiplier cost $160.35 versus the straight line.

I re-summed the 70 straight-line rows in [markets.csv](../reviews/smooth_unwind_20261125/markets.csv), checked unique market membership counts, each row's settlement and matched-share identities, and agreement with the five saved totals. A one-dollar change to a market's turnover failed the check. Net settlement was −$2,759.33. Completed FIFO pairs contributed −$2,302.71 and the remaining inventory −$456.62. Completed turnover lost in 42 markets and gained in 28. Even after omitting the three worst markets for completed turnover, the remainder lost $1,315.38 on closes. With the same 193,654 matched shares and unchanged residual, break-even would require a 1.425¢ improvement per matched share. Changing a policy changes the fills and the residual, so the experiment must measure both again.

Three comparisons guide the choice.

- **Protection against directional flow has earned its place.** Adding the existing Binance pressure guard to fixed inventory improved settlement by $7,549.69 in aggregate and helped 61 of these 70 markets. The guarded arm still lost $5,027.62. An earlier loose inventory policy actually completed +$370 of turnover, then lost $3,064 on its remaining inventory. Those arms have different feedback paths. Combining their best accounting components would overstate performance. [Paired inventory and pressure receipt](../reviews/leftover_inventory_20261125/report.md)
- **Queue priority has value, with a limited payoff in the tested policy.** On 19 earlier markets, assigning the front at activation moved the baseline-timing result from −$861 to −$659 at the endpoint. Every speed/queue combination remained negative in aggregate. Maker 3's recorded maker-to-maker closes earned 4.06¢ per matched share against our simulated −1.90¢ on the matched-market comparison. The benchmark supports looking for profitable passive cycles. Different fills, clocks and unknown posting histories preclude using it as a policy backtest. - **Taking has its own execution hurdle.** In the 1,439-market, five-day venue-matched run with a modeled 150 ms taker hold, BASE settled at −$1,118 and FLOOR at −$445. The historical delay value was unrecorded. Another session has declared a stream-triggered taking experiment. The passive experiment below measures a different action on existing resting risk. ## One proposed experiment

**Does Binance best-level depletion give us time to cancel just the inventory-adding side before a losing execution, while preserving profitable turnover?** On four consumed days, a same-price halving of the relevant Binance futures best level preceded 42% of a selected early-taker group's inferred arrivals versus 11% at placebo times. Among cases with a halving the median gap to the recorded Predict match was 235 ms. Those figures come from orders already selected for beating a delayed take. They supply a hypothesis and cannot estimate its frequency or value on our resting orders. [Strict timing diagnostic](../reviews/take_exit_venue_20261125/lead_depletion_timing_strict.json) · [Match gaps](../reviews/take_exit_venue_20261125/gap_table.json)

1. Freeze one incremental rule on the consumed October 26 baseline. At each existing decision tick compare received Binance best size with the size 300 ms earlier on the same side. Require valid, continuously observed same-price history and positive reference size. If the best ask has at most half its earlier size, suppress an otherwise eligible inventory-adding Predict YES ask. If the best bid halves, apply the mirror rule to a YES bid. Gate each tick on the observed condition. Retain the queue of unaffected orders and preserve orders that could close known inventory. Allocate reducing quantity against known holdings across both rungs, counting cancel-pending reservations. Exempt a mixed-purpose resting order if removing it would also remove protected closing quantity. Record these exemptions. Keep the existing pressure guard, opening rule, queue, size, fees, cap, unwind, 50 ms decision grid and 37 ms live cancel delay identical. Freeze recovery, missing-data and same-tick precedence in the implementation before scoring.
2. Use all decision-time eligible orders and new proposals on the reference path, including those never filled. Count unique affected generations and distinguish orders the existing guard would already remove. Compare flagged orders with 500 matched draws by market, side, remaining time, rung, queue and available quantity. Reconstruct when an actual cancel could first take effect using receipt times. Count unavoidable earlier fills, good fills forgone and later favorable fills on the same generation. Report 1 s, 5 s and 30 s markouts with coverage and full-path settlement. These attribution tables explain the outcome. Only an actual paired replay can price lost queue and changed inventory.
3. Run the original and modified feedback policies on identical tapes with a disabled-rule parity check. Show settlement dollars, completed FIFO dollars and cents/share, residual, mean absolute ending inventory, matched volume, queue time lost, both-side participation, and market-level deltas with the largest contributor removed. Zero quoting earns $0. A candidate that improves markout by avoiding nearly all trades and still loses at settlement has failed the economic objective. Stop this variant if settlement remains negative or completed turnover fails to improve. A candidate with positive settlement and better completed turnover earns a frozen follow-up on other consumed dates. A claim of profitability requires later evaluation on at least five complete untouched days with separate approval, declared coverage and venue-consistent accounting.

No strategy parameters were changed and no new markets were replayed for this review. This handoff specifies a proposed experiment, not a measured effect of the depletion rule.

## Experiment disposition, November 25

Completed [paired replay and verification](../reviews/passive_depletion_20261125/report.md). Stop the adding-side depletion override. It worsened settlement by $581.14 and completed turnover by $657.62 across the 70 reference markets. Total cancellations increased from 23,617 to 30,676. The subsequent fills on the originally flagged orders were adverse at one second, yet summed to positive contributions at settlement. Those contributions are fixed-path descriptions; the full paired policy is the relevant economic test. Retain the straight-line reference for research. The predeclared gate ends this candidate here.

## Completed-cycle spread attribution

The [baseline FIFO replay](../reviews/cycle_spread_20261125/report.md) reproduced all 70 market ledgers' checked aggregates. On matched shares the opening and closing execution prices together earned 0.991¢ versus the pre-fill mids. Signed mid movement cost 2.180¢, including 1.797¢ from opening through the earlier of one second and the close. Cycles opened by a public print at our limit earned $192.97; cycles opened by a print through it lost $2,495.68. This is a realized-path breakdown. The public print type becomes known on execution and cannot serve as a pre-fill cancellation signal.
