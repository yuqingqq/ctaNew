# Keep-or-pull S4 diagnostic: why the model pulls on false alarms (2026-11-12)

Session ce68ebe0. USER: "current model does not capture so many true positives, it still fires a lot on false [positives] ... can
you check the raw input to find out any reasons".
- Script `research/pf_kp_s4_diag.py`, run 07:25Z in the claude slot (121 s, peak 1.5 GiB).
- Post hoc: no readings, and nothing is validated on these days.
- Population: S4's 1,099,880 never-pull rows (9 replay days, M 50) with their held-out predictions. y1 = Delta at H 5 s and the 1 s
  markout, in c/sh of our 5-sh order.
- Per-input contributions are computed on a sample: 400 rows per decision group and day-side, 7,200 rows per group.

## The inputs are clean

- At most 1.3% NaN per input (act_rel, whose 30 s mean needs 20 rows at a window start), and no infinities.
- Our order's invariants hold on every row: behind is an integer, age >= 0, a0k >= 0, the smallest shown size <= 1 and <= now. The
  same check flagged all 100 planted bad rows.
- Re-predicting the held-out rows with the saved models reproduces oof.parquet exactly (max diff 0). Training and decision use the
  same feature code.
- Binance 25-100 ms returns are 97-99% zeros. That is expected for those horizons, not a defect.
- Day drift is large only for basis_bps (1.39 sd) and sigma (0.72 sd). Their contributions are small.

## Where the false pulls come from

The model pulls on 25.1% of rows:

| Decision group | Share of rows | Share of the model's pulls | Mean Delta (c/sh) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pull, nothing happens on either path within 5 s (FP0) | 18.2% | 72.5% | 0 |
| Pull, and pulling was right (TP) | 3.8% | 15.1% | -2.49 |
| Pull, but keeping was right (FP+) | 3.1% | 12.4% | +1.38 |
| Keep, but pulling was right (FN) | 11.0% | - | -1.70 |

- It catches 25.7% of the moments where pulling is right.
- **The prediction tracks activity more than harm.** Rows with any fill get higher predictions, because on average a fill makes
  keeping better (24.5% of rows favour keeping, 14.8% favour pulling).
  - Measured by AUC (0.5 = no separation), the prediction separates rows with a fill from rows without at 0.628.
  - It separates pulling-right from keeping-right, among rows with a fill, at only 0.567 (reported as 0.433 inverted).
  - So the model's lowest predictions land on quiet states, where a pull changes nothing within 5 s. On its own path, each such
    pull still re-places the order at the back of the queue.
- **What pushes the false pulls below 0 is our order's position, not the market.**
  - The largest contribution in false-pull rows comes from behind (-0.12). Those orders are at the best price (raw z -0.77), young
    (-0.45) and behind a small shown size (-0.40).
  - True pulls get the same push, and they are separated mainly by the guard value v (contribution -0.23 against -0.04, raw z
    -1.66 against -0.35 / -0.55).
  - Further separation comes from Binance's 250 ms imbalance change (-0.08 against -0.02) and its 100 ms return (-0.06 against
    -0.005): rare events.

## The label mostly measures luck on the re-entered order

- Of the moments where pulling is right:
  - only 16.6% are "our resting order would have lost and the pull path has no fill", the clean case of an adverse fill avoided;
  - 35.5% are "nothing would have happened to our order, but the re-entered order got a good fill";
  - 47.9% have fills on both paths.
- Of the moments where keeping is right, 20.6% are "nothing would have happened, but the re-entered order loses".
- Both paths lose about equally:

  | Path | Fills within 5 s | Value given a fill | Value per row |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Keep | 55% of rows | -3.02 c/sh | -1.66 |
  | Pull and re-enter | 58% of rows | -3.05 c/sh | -1.77 |

  - Delta (+0.11 on average) is the small difference of two losing lotteries.

## Raw inputs one at a time

- Among rows with a fill, the best single inputs separate harm from benefit only weakly. AUC 0.5 means no separation; the
  distances from 0.5 hold on 9 of 9 days:
  - age 0.445
  - shown size now / at activation 0.448
  - v 0.451
  - shown size 0.453
  - Binance trade count 0.544
  - Binance book age 0.458
  - activity 0.530
- Binance returns, flows and imbalances, and the Predict.fun mid changes and imbalances, are at 0.496-0.501.
  - The strong moments these inputs mark are too rare to move a population AUC. The model's bottom 1% (mean Delta -2.53) comes from
    them and from v.

## What it means

- **This is mainly a target issue, not a bug in the inputs or the fit.**
  - Keep against pull-and-re-enter compares two orders that both lose about 3 c/sh when they fill.
  - Most of its sign is decided by which of two lotteries fills, not by an adverse fill the inputs could foresee.
  - A regression on that label puts its negative predictions on quiet states.
- **The decision that carries value is "in the book or out".**
  - Resting loses 1.66 c/sh per row on average, and being out is 0.
  - That is what the guard trigger and v3 exploit: they are out most of the time.
- **A better-aligned target is the value of our resting order over the next 5 s, Q_keep against 0, without the re-entry leg.**
  - Rest only when it is predicted to be >= 0.
  - That also gives the stay-out decision directly.
  - Proposal only: same days (consumed), readings declared first, the same S4 simulation and controls.
