What each part does:
- **The age cut (Y) makes v3's fills better, not more.**
  - Per share it is better on 9 of 9 days at every fill clock.
  - It drops about a fifth of v3's fills, the ones worth about nothing, so $/day is unchanged.
  - At M 250 it loses far less (-2.9 against -11.9 $/day, better on 9 of 9 days, post hoc). This is the one change that makes v3
    less dependent on the unknown fill clock.
- **Keeping through flicker (K, K2) hurts.** It adds fills that lose per share. The flicker cancels protect v3, as B15 found for
  the live soft pull. Drop it.
- **Joining new levels at 30 ms (F30) adds money, not quality.**
  - +8.6 $/day at M 50 and +11.3 at M 0, on 9 of 9 days. Per share it is slightly below v3.
  - At M 250 it loses more than v3 (-14.0 against -11.9).
- **Y + F30 is the best combination.**
  - v3's per-share value (+1.05 against +1.03 at M 50) at 1.6 times the fills.
  - +8.7 $/day at M 50, day-clustered 95% interval [+6.2, +11.3] (post hoc).
  - Less loss at M 250 than v3 (-5.6 against -11.9; 7 of 9 days, post hoc).

## What it means

- **At today's speed, joining earlier does not make more money.** The age cut is still worth keeping: same money, better fills,
  far less exposure if trades match 250 ms before they are reported.
- **The money is in speed:** joining a new level within about 30 ms of its birth, while the guard is on. Today's median is 97 ms.
  - 30 ms is below our 53 ms median feed delay alone.
  - It needs a faster Predict.fun feed (the venue's smallest delay is 13.2 ms) and a faster order path than our 40 ms.
  - QR showed birth joins at 55 and 97 ms earn less than at 30 ms. v3 + F at 55 / 97 ms is not tested.
- **Do not keep orders through guard flicker.**
