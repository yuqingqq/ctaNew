Do wide spreads explain the profits? All 19 markets

Gross positive completed net turnover is grouped by the book spread at both matched legs. Losses and unmatched inventory are retained separately. This is descriptive attribution, not proof of a quoting rule.

Pre-window and upper-bound books can disagree because trades move the book and match timestamps are uncertain. The “mainly wide” screen requires both legs wider than 4¢ to account for over half the positive completed value on both clocks.

Current inventory controls

Quantity skew shrinks the risk-adding side; it does not shift quote prices. In the wider test, bid multiplier = clip(1 − I/500,0,1), ask multiplier = clip(1 + I/500,0,1). Maximum order size100, desired side exposure200.

At +500, risk-adding bids are withheld and resting bids requested for cancellation; the restriction releases at +250. Negative inventory is symmetric. The1,000 hard cap counts outstanding and cancel-pending reservations. The prior baseline used100 /50 /200.

Pressure/structural rules remain active. The separate active-take test did not trigger on the three wider-band paths. Size10 on an adding side is entirely possible: at I=+450, proposed bid10 versus reducing ask100 before reservation clipping.
