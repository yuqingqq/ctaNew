# Take signal and break-even maker exit — independent review, 2026-11-25

Reviewed result commit 23282c33b600a34c7ca5f4d7210f5608d60be64c and its inherited STRICT58 execution model. The subsequent manifest generator 56314c5c2 records the rule. The result is reproducible development evidence for the simulated policy on 408 complete markets. The execution and population evidence is insufficient for a live return estimate.

## Flow and reproduction

On a 50 ms grid the policy measures the signed 250 ms Binance return using an assumed feed delay. An upside crossing buys YES; a downside crossing buys NO in the YES-equivalent book. Each side requires no ON signal in its prior two seconds of available history, with the history reset at market start. The IOC is limited to the received touch, up to 50 shares with a 50-share directional cap and reservations. At its modeled 58 ms arrival, the engine checks venue price, displayed quantity net of earlier use and trades, and the next frame. That later frame is an execution assumption; the trigger does not see it. After a 100 ms fill notice the strategy can place a passive closing order. For a long, FLOOR asks at the higher of the current ask and rounded-up fee-inclusive average cost; for a short, it uses the lower bid and rounded-down proceeds. Pressure and structural cancels still apply, and an opposite signal can take at a loss. After 298 s, the remaining position settles. See take_replay.py:60, 81–109, other_days.py:135, strict_rerun.py:35–73, exit_replay.py:35–86, floor_replay.py:43–74,105–136,156–180.

I checked the hashes and distinct market identities of all 816 BASE/FLOOR ledgers. Independently accumulated signed quantities, cash, taker fees, order limits and IOC timing from each fill, checked the registry outcome, and reconciled to each published total under its payout convention. After tie correction BASE is **+$1,880.64**, FLOOR **+$2,360.47**, and the difference is **+$479.83**. Both the total and the difference are positive on each day. Under the recorded fee convention, completed turnover improves by $703.87 while settlement carry deteriorates by roughly $224. Aggregate absolute carried shares increase from 1,242.8 to 2,284.6. The floor changes subsequent inventory, takes, and exits; the comparison prices that whole feedback path.

## Ranked findings

1. **Effective taker latency is the largest execution uncertainty.** STRICT58 hardcodes 58 ms on every take (floor_replay.py:186–197). The historical clock calibration provides order-live timestamps, with the reported probe using far-from-touch orders. It does not establish distributional time to *marketable matching* on these instruments (pf_clock_rl.py:8–18; EXPLORATION_2026-11-01.md §21). Before arrival there is only a fixed feed-delay shift. After arrival, the two-frame cap uses quantity remaining or printed at the level. Replacement liquidity can appear between frames, and competitor arrival order is unobserved (strict_rerun.py:51–71). Thus it is a useful sensitivity with ambiguous fill error in individual cases. Current venue documentation specifies immediate cancellation of unfilled MARKET quantity and a possible market-specific taker delay. In the first complete market of each reviewed day, the archived category market object omitted speedBump. The sample establishes neither a historical bump value nor its absence. The earlier BASE stress changing 40 ms/lenient to 58 ms/strict cut marked value from $5,923 to $1,902; that is a joint timing and fill-rule sensitivity. [Venue order and speed-bump rules](https://dev.predict.fun/trading/place-orders)
2. **Forward availability and maker exits limit the floor conclusion.** Just 408/1,440 scheduled markets entered this paired test (546 missing endpoints, 485 stale future books, one forward boundary). The complete-market decision uses later book availability; no entry-time selector reproduces that census (floor_replay.py:281–297,373–382). V2 already measured smaller take edges on excluded markets. The five days were previously consumed and the floor was proposed after the base results were known. Exit activation/cancel use 10 ms, and the queue ahead can fall with displayed depth (pf_branch_engine.py:119–134,153–171). Under the corrected payout a direct fixed-fill valuation against settlement attributes +$319.25 to FLOOR takes net of fees and +$2,041.22 to its passive fills, versus +$637.41 and +$1,243.22 for BASE. These are accounting contributions: a deletion/replay can change every later trade. This cuts against attributing all five-day profits independently to taking. Of floor-arm completed turnover, only $27.52 was realized on exits initially posted away at the floor; most maker-close dollars came from exits posted at the touch (fill_split.py:21–54). A profitable floor in aggregate does not prove that each delayed exit or floor-priced order earns the improvement.
3. **Native order minima and fee denomination affect executable quantities.** take_replay.py:88–108 and floor_replay.py:163–180 enforce share/price bounds. Under the native BUY YES / BUY NO mapping, 57 of 7,941 FLOOR take submissions and 664 of 7,602 passive submissions fall below the published 1 USDT buy minimum, checked on submitted quantity rather than partial fill size. Those orders supplied 386.46 taker and 1,275.05 maker shares in the replay. An inventory-backed native sale has different minima and needs sellable tokens. The fee amount in dollars reconciles, but buying removes tokens while the simulation credits the full shares and debits cash. Under complementary buys on both sides, valuing token fees at the actual payout reduces FLOOR by $69.44 and BASE by $77.62 holding every fill fixed; actual exit quantity, break-even rounding and later fills require a replay. Recorded FLOOR had 21 passive fills that increased absolute net position even though posts are closing when proposed, consistent with fills racing delayed position updates. The policy guarantees closing-side intent at submission. [Venue order rules](https://dev.predict.fun/trading/place-orders) · [Fee denomination](https://dev.predict.fun/trading/fees)
4. **Settlement has a small real label error.** floor_replay.py:205–209 accepts only 0/1; pf_stop_ev_minutes_inputs.py:49 maps the registry Up string to 1. Eight included markets have equal start and end references and are labelled Up. The retained historical description for 3667409 explicitly pays 50/50 on equality, consistent with the venue rules and the prior pf_mt_econ.py:313–335. Repricing ties reduces FLOOR by $1.31 and its advantage by $0.23. This fix can be bundled without repeating the strategy search. [Resolution rules](https://dev.predict.fun/concepts/resolution)
