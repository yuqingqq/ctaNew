# Passive depletion v1
One development candidate. Reference is the buffered linear curve on exactly the same 70 complete consumed October 26 markets. Economic gate is positive total settlement and improved completed FIFO turnover over that reference; otherwise stop. Cash is in USD. All unchanged baseline assumptions and exclusions apply. No fitting, further days, or parameter search.

Use raw Binance bookTicker recorder receipt times. At each 50 ms decision tick compare the same best price continuously since the observation as of 300 ms ago. A size of at most half activates only that side. Require a valid uncrossed book throughout, both endpoints fresh within 300 ms and no inter-receipt gaps over 300 ms between endpoints. Price changes, refresh or size recovery clear the flag on the next grid. Unknown signal delegates. Existing structural, inventory, pressure and opening decisions take precedence. Protect closing quantity using known position, retiring reservations first and then older remaining orders. Keep mixed live orders intact; cut new mixed proposals to the executable reducing quantity, exempting a mixed proposal whose reducing part alone is below venue minimum. A withheld opening does not start the market. Other-side proposals and fills follow the original engine.

Primary full-path metrics are settlement, FIFO completed and residual money, matched quantity, expiry exposure and participation. Record submission/cancellation counts, age of actually cancelled queue positions and future reference resting time removed from the first flagged live opportunity. The latter is an exposure measure at fixed future public flow. Per-market disabled-arm metrics must reproduce the existing linear replay.

Reference diagnostics count all eligible calls, statuses and incremental flags. Attribute an entire generation once, at its first purely adding flagged live review or submission. Mixed new clips contribute to the paired policy alone. Include refused and never-filled proposals in the reference generation sample. Separate fills already matched by a live decision and those still in flight. Fills at the live cancel effect are removable; new slots are valued from activation. Signed positive and negative cash contributions use each fill's settlement price difference or its +1/+5/+30 s midpoint difference. Mark missing when past END, invalid or more than 1 s stale. A setting's first flags describe a fixed path; the paired replay prices all later entries and exits.

500 control draws, seed 20261125. In each market sample one uniform eligible unflagged decision per generation as a potential control. Match on live/slot, side, birth/proposed rung, minute left, queue ahead (unknown/zero/up to/beyond remaining quantity) and size at most/above 110 shares. For slots use received displayed depth; for resting orders use replay queue as an explanatory matching dimension only. Exclude same-stratum treated IDs from controls. When controls are scarce draw an equally sized random treated subset once and disclose support; sample controls without replacement within stratum per draw. Compare signed settlement contribution. These are descriptive draws within one consumed day. No confidence intervals. Dropping the largest positive and largest absolute settlement contributor gives concentration sensitivities.
