How to read this evidence

This is a microscope, not a validation result. Three previously discussed markets were chosen before this extraction. They illustrate different paths; they do not establish how often a pattern works.

Our current arm is the saved CURRENT_ASYNC_FAST10 replay: two 100-share levels, 200-share inventory/reservation cap, inventory hysteresis and Binance-pressure guard. The alternate arm assumes every newly accepted order starts ahead of external queue. It is a complete hypothetical feedback path, not an estimate of a top maker’s queue.

Our queue is sampled at activation-book initialization, first live review and pressure pull. It is not interpolated. A maker’s unfilled orders, private cancellations and continuous queue rank are not identified. Dashed post windows are scenario estimates; subsequent fills prove the same order traded again, not that we observed its entire resting path.

Markouts are gross cents per filled share and are not completed spread capture. Our 1/5/30-second values use saved simulator mids; maker 1/10/60-second values use accepted recorded-fill valuation. Do not compare unmatched horizons as if identical. Curves sample 100–200 ms; accounting uses every included fill. Stale or invalid prices are gaps.

Reproduction and checks: report · artifact checks · input hashes. No new strategy runs, new days, fitted model, or live actions.
