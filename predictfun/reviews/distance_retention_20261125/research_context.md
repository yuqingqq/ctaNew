# How the passive experiments fit together

The current reference quotes two levels of up to 220 shares per side with a 1,000-share hard limit. It waits for information at the opening, uses the 250 ms Binance pressure guard and gradually reduces inventory room into expiry. Decisions use client-known holdings and reservations. Settlement, completed FIFO value, residual inventory, filled volume and queue participation belong together when evaluating a change.

| Decision | What the evidence supports |
|---|---|
| Keep the existing pressure guard | On 70 complete October 26 markets, fixed inventory lost $12,577 without pressure protection and $5,028 with it. 61 markets improved. The older four-day ablation found cancellation and withholding each contributed to the policy improvement, although all arms lost at their pre-expiry endpoint. The four-day historical ladder results use smaller quotes and pre-expiry mid marks. The 70-market November 25 studies replay October 26 and settle the binary position. The taker experiment and the top-maker studies have their own populations and fee conventions. Their dollar totals cannot be combined, and this research supplies no estimate of future profit.

## Decision after the paired test

Keeping both distant rungs produced **+$1,890.96 at settlement**, across 42 positive and 28 other markets, but failed the frozen exposure criterion. Mean absolute final inventory rose to 186.2 shares versus the 51.9-share acceptance ceiling. Full feedback changes the entry and exit prices, which cycles close, which positions remain outstanding, and available quote slots. The 7.00¢ per matched share is conditional on closing. The −$4,046.95 residual is necessary to interpret it. A through-price opening also changed from loss-making in aggregate to profitable among the cycles that closed, so a rule that classifies all sweeps as bad would discard some profitable business under the new policy. [Paired replay](report.md)

**Next priority:** one role-specific distance rule on the same consumed population. At a distance-only cancellation, preserve a quote that would only increase inventory. Let the existing distance rule replace a quote that can close client-known inventory, with priority given to older closing orders after reserving shares for cancellations already in flight. Treat a mixed-purpose quote as a potential close. Keep the two slots, current price limits, full pressure protection, end-of-market inventory curve and passive execution. Read the comparison against both controls (the original and unconditional retention) before changing another parameter. Require positive settlement and completed turnover, higher settlement and completed P&L than the original, and average absolute expiry holdings within the original ten-share tolerance. Report matched shares, both fill types, residual losses, market concentration, and how often a released reducing slot actually reposts and closes before expiry. This distinguishes the benefit of patient entries from the cost of leaving exits behind. Whether that specific trade-off explains the observed inventory increase remains untested. Any passing candidate still requires other dates before a profitability claim.
