**Two corrections, and one of them is a gap.**

- **It is five keys, not four**, and the instrument does **not** waive them: `asm.assembly` is
  recorded `allowed_to_differ: false` and reported under `DATA_DIFFERS`. The judgement "these

- **Two of the five are elided and cannot be checked.** `bn_cache_clear_s` (4.963→5.079),

  plainly clocks. **`score_s_by_head` and `stages` are recorded only as `"<dict len=2>"` on

  the ones that could carry something other than a clock, and `stages` is a name that in the
  receipt denotes resource telemetry with `baseline_gb`/`budget_gb`, not timings.

**So: the allowed-different set is exactly provenance + clocks for the three keys I can see,
and unverified for the two I cannot.** I would not sign the "only clocks" half until BE prints
the leaf diff for `score_s_by_head` and `stages` — key path, landed value, rebuilt value — as

round unpickles nothing.

**Keys I would not allow to differ** (none of them did, and this is the list I would hold):
