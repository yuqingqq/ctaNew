# ITEM 2 — **the adjudication: 20/20 is a real property, and DE's own record says it is structural rather than a budget shortfall**

**P4 is a real predicate, not one that can only fail.** Both directions exist in committed code: `de_phase4_diag_runner.py:4083` asserts `P4_realised_action_counts_equal` **true** in a positive control, and `:4124` asserts it **false** on a constructed known-bad. So a refusal at P4 is evidence about the draw, not an artefact of a predicate that never admits.

**So 20/20 is a real property of this control on this population — and the mechanism is one DE already recorded, two rounds before it hit it again.** `de_phase4_diag_runner.py:4126-4129`, in the repository since round 36:

> *"a permutation that changes the per-stratum realised action count is REJECTED and redrawn … because **a stateful policy cannot be made to cancel exactly the drawn set** — which is why `control#2` is withdrawn (DE36-R3)."*

**That changes the reading.** Round 54 attributes the failure to the budget — *"matching the per-stratum REALISED counts across that gap is not something the frozen 20-attempt budget can hit"* — which reads as bad luck at a chosen n. The numbers say otherwise: the treated arm realises **333 of 1,154** above events (28.9 %) and the control realised **496** (43.0 %) on the first seed. That is a large, one-directional gap, not dispersion around a matched target, and a larger budget does not close a systematic offset.

**The structural point, which I think is the finding rather than the 20/20:** P2 and P3 require the draw to match the treated arm's **above-event** multiset per stratum; P4 requires it to match the **realised-action** count per stratum. Under a stateful policy — where a held side suppresses later crossings — realisation is a function of the draw's *order*, so the two requirements are over-determined. Satisfying P2/P3 essentially fixes what P4 will be, and it will not in general be the treated arm's number.

If that is right, then `VALID_AS_A_CONTROL` can never be True **for this construction**, and `floor_available: false` is a statement about the design rather than about this population. That is a decision to take — whether P4 is the right conjunct for a stateful policy, or whether the matched floor needs a different definition — and it is not a decision another 20 draws can inform.

**My adjudication: the refusal is correct, the 20/20 is real, and the programme should stop drawing and rule on P4.** Refusing rather than reporting a weaker control was the right call and I would not want it relaxed.
