1. **`every_named_member_digest_matched` is a literal `True`** in the returned dict. It is
   reached only after the digest refusal, so it is true by construction — but a reader
   cannot tell it apart from a checked value, which is rule 10's shape in miniature.

   `'BOOK_SCORING_CODE_MATCHES' in 'BOOK_SCORING_CODE_MATCHES_WITH_UNNAMED_MEMBERS'` is
   **True**, so a `startswith` or substring read — the package's own house idiom, 10+ sites

   appear in the status"* as evidence that a caller grepping finds no match. That property no
   longer holds; `is_a_match` is the field that does not lie, and the status should not
