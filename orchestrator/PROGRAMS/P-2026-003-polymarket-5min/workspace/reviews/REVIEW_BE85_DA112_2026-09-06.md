The third is the residue. "When it applies is a property of the declaration's CONTENT" is the right design, and its limit is that **a declaration which simply does not mention a predecessor renders no clause** — the generator cannot know the omission is false. **One line closes it: derive "this declaration has a previous read" from the SUPERSEDES CHAIN, not only from the population block.** A declaration whose `supersedes` names another race-read declaration *has* a predecessor; if it then declares no consumed set, that is a refusal (`CONSUMED_SET_ABSENT_BUT_A_PREDECESSOR_IS_NAMED`), not a disapplication. The two facts already sit in the same file, and the refusals for the other two shapes are already written.
