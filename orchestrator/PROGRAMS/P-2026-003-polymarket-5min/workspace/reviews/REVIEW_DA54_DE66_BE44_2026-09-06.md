### A.1a **FINDING — what a SIGKILL leaves, and why the `Killed` marker cannot catch it**

The failure-marker regex is
`^Traceback|^[A-Za-z_.]*(Error|Exception):|Killed|Segmentation fault|MemoryError`.
I built the case and ran it:

```
python3 kt.py > kill.log 2>&1      # prints MASK_STATUS=CONTENT_LIVENESS_UNJUDGEABLE, then SIGKILLs itself
bash: line 1: 2703820 Killed       python3 kt.py > kill.log 2>&1     <- the PARENT shell's stderr
rc=137
--- kill.log (41 bytes) ---
MASK_STATUS=CONTENT_LIVENESS_UNJUDGEABLE
```

**The log contains the token and nothing else.** The word `Killed` is written by
**bash into the parent shell's stderr**, not by the killed child into the
redirected file — so `> "$_mlog" 2>&1` never captures it. Conjunct (a) is
satisfied, conjunct (b) finds no marker, and the classification is **DEFERRED**,
mapped to rc 2, reported as success.

**Severity: narrow but invisible.** The kill must land *after* the except-branch
printed — a small window, and the common case (killed during `build_mask`) emits
no token at all and correctly fails. **But `Killed` in that regex is dead code:
it can essentially never match**, which is worth knowing on its own.

**Cheap fix, using a signal the shell already has and the log never will:** the
unit captures `_mrc` (`:386–389`) and currently treats rc 1 and rc 137
identically. **Require `_mrc == 1` — the builder's own refusal return — for
DEFERRED; any other non-zero rc is a FAILURE.** rc is outside the log and cannot
be forged by log content at all.

## B.3 **ATTACK on the DE 67 ruling — per-arm rates are the RIGHT NULL and the WRONG SHARED DENOMINATOR**

**Where the ruling is right.** A rate replayed over the population a cancel
decision is actually drawn from, **matched to that arm's own decision count and
side mix**, is what CLAUDE.md rule 7 demands of a control. And it reconstructs
BE's own measured ratios exactly — recomputing DE's arms against BE's per-arm
means gives **8.7013** and **5.5684**, which are BE's 8.70× and 5.57×. As a
*null for one arm*, it is the best of the three.

**Where it breaks, and it is not a matter of taste.** `cascade_spread` is
invariant to the baseline **only because the baseline is SHARED**:

```
shared:   (n_C/B)  / (n_H/B)   = n_C/n_H          <- B cancels
per-arm:  (n_C/B_C)/ (n_H/B_H) = (n_C/n_H)·(B_H/B_C)   <- it does not
```

Computed, not argued:

```
cascade_spread now (book-wide)      = 1.9398838090426878
under BE's per-arm baselines        = 1.5626296          (-19.4%)
old_spread x (B_H/B_C = 0.8055274)  = 1.5626296          <- the identity holds exactly
```

**So the invariance DE certified last round — `cascade_spread` identical under
both book-wide baselines, which is what makes "CHEAP FILLS FIRST, FEW FILLS
SECOND" baseline-proof — holds only for shared denominators and is LOST the
moment per-arm rates are adopted.** The ordering may well survive on the
recomputed numbers, but **its robustness argument does not carry over and must be
re-derived, not inherited.**

**And the field is book-wide by name and by position.** `fills_per_generation`
sits at the `cancel_mechanics` level, not inside an arm. **Writing a per-arm rate
into it is a category error at the schema level even when the number is the
better null.**

**What the cascade factor would MEAN under the ruling:** *"fills removed per
cancel, relative to a random policy matched to THIS arm's own decision count and
side mix."* That is an **arm-local** statistic — legitimate, better-conditioned,
and **not a cross-arm comparator**. Today's book-wide version means *"relative to
what the book runs per generation"*, which is comparable across arms and worse as
a null. **They answer different questions and the ruling silently swaps one for
the other in a field named for the first.**

**Recommendation:** keep `fills_per_generation` book-wide and mark it
`NOT_THE_NULL`; put BE's per-arm rates in the per-arm blocks, where DE already
has the slots (`cascade_factor_vs_filling_generation_rate`,
`cascade_factor_vs_random_decision_rate`); and either **stop emitting a cross-arm
`cascade_spread` under per-arm denominators or emit both with their denominators
named**. Keep citing BE by commit/digest as `be_replayed_random_null_4c17646`
already does, so DE's number cannot move when BE re-runs.
