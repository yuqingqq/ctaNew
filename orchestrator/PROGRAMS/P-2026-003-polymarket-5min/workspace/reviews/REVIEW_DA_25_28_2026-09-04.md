### DA28-R1 — MEDIUM — the docstring's justification for the floor does not reproduce, and contradicts DA's own selftest

`covered_complement`'s docstring argues the floor is not delicate:

> *"Measured across the record the choice is not delicate: coverage runs 08-29 288, **08-30 66, 08-31 0**, 09-01 288, 09-02 288, 09-03 287, so there is a natural gap between 99.65 % and 23 % and **ANY floor in that range admits 09-03 and nothing else**."*

Two problems, both measured:

1. **08-30 and 08-31 have 288 covered windows each**, by my file census *and* by the verdict's own instrument. The figure 66 is not coverage — it is the number of windows **before 08-30's era boundary** (05:30:02Z → 66 windows), which I confirmed by computing it. 08-31's boundary at 22:00:02Z splits 264/24, so the quoted **0** is not that quantity either and does not reproduce as anything I can find.
2. Consequently **the "natural gap" does not exist** in the quantity the floor is applied to. The real coverage distribution is 115 · 279 · 287 · 288×12, so the gap is between **279 and 115** — and **at the floor actually used (144), 08-26's `complete_tape` flips too**. "ANY floor in that range admits 09-03 and nothing else" is false at 144, which is precisely what DA's selftest DA28-7 asserts 3,300 lines later in the same file.

**The conclusion survives — I verified it directly — but the sentence that defends the floor against the charge of having been chosen to fit 09-03 is wrong.** That sentence is rule-11 load-bearing: it is the argument that the bound is natural rather than fitted. The honest version is available and stronger: *the floor admits two days' coverage predicates, 09-03 and 08-26, and 08-26 is excluded by the freeze regardless — so no floor in a wide range changes any admission.*

> **Clause.** Replace the coverage series with the measured one (115 / 279 / 287 / 288) and state the reach as it is: two `complete_tape` flips, one admission. Nothing else changes.
