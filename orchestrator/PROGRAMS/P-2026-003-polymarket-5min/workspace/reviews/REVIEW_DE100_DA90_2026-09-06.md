## 1.2 The `-E` runtime check — what a parent cmdline hides

`parent_is_flock` is `Path(argv[0]).name == "flock"`. Driven:

```

argv[0] = 'flock'      /proc/<pid>/exe = '/usr/bin/sleep'      parent_is_flock -> True
```

**`argv[0]` is a self-report; `/proc/<pid>/exe` is not.** The check reads the claim where the property is available at the same cost. ## 2.3 The open-book known-bads — what a pickle can do that a shape check never sees

The three refusals are the right shape and the right order, and the light path refuses by name before any load. What none of them can see is that **`pickle.load` executes the payload's own opcodes before any shape check runs**: `__reduce__` on an untrusted pickle is arbitrary code, and the shape check runs *after* the object exists. Here the pickle is BE's, written by a sibling seat in the same repo, so the exposure is not an adversary — it is **the same class as reading another seat's book: the reader inherits whatever the writer's process could do**, and "safely" cannot mean "validated after loading". ## 4.1 The strongest case AGAINST R-656's ruling on the counts

The ruling: `n_decisions`, `n_fills_baseline`, `n_fills_arm`, `n_cancels_issued` are decision-population **sizes**, outside the seal, "rule 8's n". The strongest case against it, in three steps:

1. **The justification covers two of the four names.** `n_decisions` and `n_scored_rows` are population sizes and rule 8 requires them. **`n_fills_arm`, `n_fills_baseline` and `n_cancels_issued` are not sizes of a population — they are counts of an OUTCOME under a policy.** The difference between the arm's fills and the baseline's is the intervention's effect measured in events. Calling all four "n" collapses a size and a result under one word.
2. **What a reader of six days' open counts could choose after seeing.** Any remaining degree of freedom becomes choosable on a quantity monotone in the intervention: whether to re-run a refused day, which arm is primary, and — the sharp one — **whether the sixth day lands before the 12:00Z horizon or the read opens at G = 5 directional** (R-604's own fallback). Nobody need intend it; rule 11 forbids the *possibility*, not the motive, and the possibility exists as soon as a per-day directional proxy is readable before the read.
3. **The receipt's own sentence is stronger than the ruling.** `seal_status` says whoever runs the remaining days "**has not seen this one's result**". A reader who can order the two arms by intervention size on day 1 has seen something about the result. Either that sentence narrows to "no economic field", or the outcome counts move behind the seal.

**The fair counter, and the actionable form:** `n_decisions` **must** stay open — R4's admissibility bar is checked per day before the read, and sealing it would make the pre-read impossible. So the split is not four-versus-zero: **keep the population sizes open (the bar reads them); seal the three outcome counts, which no pre-read conjunct consults**, or state in design v22 exactly why an intervention-size proxy is not part of "the result". I am not ruling; I am saying the ruling's stated basis does not reach the three names that carry the information.
