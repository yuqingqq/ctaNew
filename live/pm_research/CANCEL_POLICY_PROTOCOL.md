### 1.2 Estimand

For each fill, the **warning window** `W` = time from the **first envelope
event** of the fill's resting episode (post or re-post to fill) to the fill.
Envelope events are deliberately **parameter-free maximal supersets** of the
trigger family — no trigger-parameter choice can shape them:

- `E-FLOW`: any aggressive same-side trade at our level or better;
- `E-DEPLETE`: any decrease in displayed depth ahead-of/at our level since the
  (re)post;
- `E-MID`: any adverse mid move ≥ 1 tick since the (re)post.

`W` is measured in event time. A fill with **no** envelope event in its episode
is **UNWARNED** and belongs to the `W ≤ lag` bucket by definition. Both queue
bounds (`FRONT` / `BACK_DISPLAYED`) are computed and reported — the bounds
change which trades count as "ahead", hence `E-FLOW`/`E-DEPLETE` timing.

### 1.3 Report

Per coin × queue bound × micro-class arm: the `W` distribution (p10/25/50/75/90,
unwarned share), and the **rescuable-drift share**
`R(τ) = Σ negative drift on fills with W > lag + τ ÷ Σ negative drift`,
`lag = 250 ms`, per rung `τ ∈ {0, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1000} ms`. Drift at
`h = 5 s` primary (btc drift −1.175 ¢, eth −2.021 ¢ baselines), `h = 15, 30`
secondary. **Receipt-key mapping, pinned (iteration 5): the receipt's
`bounds.join` arm IS `BACK_DISPLAYED` and `bounds.front` IS `FRONT`** — the
join arm rests behind displayed depth (≈346 fills/window btc vs ≈1,868 front:
the **~5× fill-count ratio**, a different quantity from the corpus's 9.4×
front-of-queue INVENTORY-risk ratio — one mechanism, two statistics, named
apart per iteration 6), and it is the higher-`R`, generous arm, as the frozen
§1.4 rationale predicts. **`R` is a DESCRIPTIVE point estimate with no
valid day-clustered interval — the `edge_l1_v1` receipt stamps `source_days` n=4, and that field
counts days READ; the measured sample is ONE UTC day
(`FLOW_MODEL_STATE.md` §1f). Under either count there is at most a handful of
day clusters and in fact one: neither §1.4 verdict is inferential; both are
structural readings of an upper bound (Ruling R-1).** Window-clustered CIs
reported as descriptive spread; day-clustered not computable, not claimed.

### 1.4 Branch rule — **FROZEN by Ruling R-1 (`COORDINATION.md` §4a, 2026-08-23)**

**The derivation is accepted.** `f* = |markout| / |drift|` is exactly
`1 − spread/|drift|`, the fraction of post-fill drift that must be excised to
bring a never-cancelling maker back to break-even, granted zero capture loss
and the generous queue bound. It is a genuine falsifier: it is an UPPER-bound
test, so failing it kills the family rather than merely disappointing it.

**Why `BACK_DISPLAYED` is the generous arm here (recorded so a later reader
cannot mistake it for a copy error from the fill-side convention):** the
pessimistic FILL bound is the optimistic WARNING bound — filling only on
sweeps lengthens the resting episode and therefore `W`.

**AMENDED at freeze — the bar carries the interval it was derived from.**
Freezing `f*` at a point estimate would let a `DEAD` verdict fire inside the
noise of the bar itself. `markout` is `−0.532 [−0.797, −0.287]` on btc and
`−1.243 [−1.726, −0.759]` on eth; spread capture is the stable leg
(+0.642 / +0.778), so the interval propagates through the identity
`|drift| = spread + |markout|`:

| coin | `f*` low | `f*` point | `f*` high |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| btc | **30.9 %** | 45.3 % | **55.4 %** |
| eth | **49.4 %** | 61.5 % | **68.9 %** |

**Three-way verdict, per coin, on `R(τ=250 ms)` under `BACK_DISPLAYED`:**

- `R < f*_low` → **DEAD.** Below break-even under the most forgiving reading
  of the bar. No parameterised rule rescues the family on that coin.
- `R ≥ f*_high` → **GO.**
- otherwise → **INDETERMINATE.** The family is **not** killed: §2 runs on that
  coin, and the grid's candidate bar must clear `f*_high`, not `f*_point`.
  Recorded as not-established, never as a pass.

`INDETERMINATE` is a real outcome with a real consequence, not a way of
declining the question. `R(τ=0)` is reported beside the decision rung; a
lag-floor failure is reported WITH ITS MARGIN (R-9 corrected the frozen
text's own "doubly dead" shorthand in how results are phrased: say "dead at
the decision rung, and additionally at the lag floor with margin X" — the
2026-08-20 btc lag-floor margin is 1.2 pp, thin, while every decision-rung
margin is wide). **VOID**: fewer than 500 fills
with valid drift and computable `W` on a coin at `h=5`. **Family verdict:**
DEAD only if BOTH verdict coins are DEAD.

**Verification note (R-1's own instruction, executed before applying):** the
six numbers were checked against `derived/edge_layer1_v1.json`. The receipt
equals the published figures at their stated precision — no disagreement, no
STOP. One rounding-provenance detail, disclosed rather than silently
recomputed: full-precision receipt propagation gives btc `f*_low` = 30.85 %
(the frozen 30.9 % comes from the published 3-dp figures); the frozen bar sits
0.04 pp on the *harsher-on-the-family* side of the full-precision value, so it
cannot flatter a pass. Tie-break, pre-stated: if `R(τ=250 ms)` on btc lands
inside [30.85 %, 30.9 %), report BOTH readings to the coordinator rather than
picking one. All other five values agree at 0.1 pp under both precisions.

### 1.5 Controls (selftest must fail if the probe is vacuous)

1. Synthetic episode with a known envelope event exactly Δ before a synthetic
   fill → `W = Δ` exactly.
2. Synthetic episode with no event → `UNWARNED`, `W ≤ lag` bucket.
3. A shuffle control, implemented as: permute the fills' `W` values within
   each window (fixed seed) and recompute `R(τ)` — preserves both marginals
   while breaking the fill↔drift association, so a measured `R` profile that
   the shuffle reproduces would mean `R` reflects `W`'s density, not which
   fills are warned. Reported beside `R` in the receipt. *(Amended blind
   before any receipt was read, to match the implemented control exactly —
   `warning_window.py::shuffled_r`.)*

## 2. Step 2 — the cancellation replay grid (`cancel_v1`) — runs only on GO, only after freeze

### 2.1 Grid (every cell reported; none selected in-sample)

| dimension | values |
|---|---|
| trigger | `T-FLOW(k ∈ {0, 1} ticks)` · `T-DEPLETE(floor ∈ {25 %, 50 %} of depth-ahead at post)` · `T-MID(m ∈ {1, 2} ticks)` |
| effective latency | `lag + τ`, `τ ∈ {0, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1000} ms`; fills before `t + lag + τ` still happen |
| cooldown (event triggers) | `{1, 5, 15} s` for `T-FLOW`; state triggers re-arm on predicate clear |
| re-post | re-join back of the **current** same-side touch (SKEW_LB convention); cancel-and-stay-out as a labelled diagnostic bound only |
| queue bound | `FRONT` and `BACK_DISPLAYED` |
| book | static two-sided `JOIN` first; composed skew × cancel second (policy plan §8.3) |

`T-AGE` is excluded with cause (policy plan §4.3: waiting never worsens queue
position; its rationale was backwards).

### 2.2 Accounting (per cell)

Three axes plus the flip axis, all mandatory: retained-fill count and spread
capture; fill-conditional markout at `h ∈ {5, 15, 30, 60}`; terminal `|net|` +
side-aware cash at risk; **cancel/re-post/flip counts** (replay prices flips at
zero — the count is the honest proxy). **Excised fills carry counterfactual
markout rows** (the avoided fill's subsequent mid path is known); **partial
fill then cancel** is a named row type (filled part retained, remainder
excised, one order). Triggers read the tape strictly before the fill at
knowledge time through the frozen lag — no look-ahead. Standing rules: per
coin, R-DUAL, `UNAVAILABLE` rows, provenance, window-clustered CIs, selftest
controls incl. a synthetic tape where the trigger provably excises a known
fill.

### 2.3 Candidate bar — PROPOSED for freeze (amended by R-1)

A cell is a **CANDIDATE** iff, on a verdict coin:

1. retained-fill markout at `h = 5` is positive with the window-clustered CI
   excluding zero;
2. retained spread capture ≥ 50 % of the never-cancel baseline's capture;
3. both hold under BOTH queue bounds at `τ ≥ 250 ms`;
4. **(R-1 amendment)** on a coin whose §1.4 verdict was `INDETERMINATE`, the
   cell's excised-drift share clears **`f*_high`**, not `f*_point` — recorded
   as not-established, never as a pass.

Candidates are DEVELOPMENT. **Promotion** needs ≥ 10 complete forward days the
cell was not tuned on, executed as the registered `RulePolicy_v1` plugin under
EV-Replay (a harness transcription can develop, not promote), with the same
bars holding. No PnL or capacity claim at any stage; Layer 1 and Layer 2 stay
separately accounted.

### 2.4 What would kill the grid mid-run

Nothing — the grid always completes and reports every cell. Interpretive kills
happen only at the frozen bars: all cells failing §2.3 = the lever is dead on
this tape at these parameter ranges, reported as such with the excised
counterfactuals that show *why* (non-selective excision vs insufficient
warning vs capture destruction).
